## Feel bilingual from day one.

LingoBlend brings the vocabulary you are learning out of flashcards and into the webpages you are already browsing. It blends target-language words from your own word bank into native-language pages, creating a playful form of bilingual code-switching while you browse.

A word can stay an answer on a flashcard—something you can translate when asked. Or it can become a word that simply makes sense when you see it.

LingoBlend is built for the second kind of familiarity.

## Words that belong in your life

A language you want to learn should not exist only during lessons, streaks, or review sessions. LingoBlend lets your target-language vocabulary appear in the articles, communities, hobbies, stories, and everyday sites you already enjoy.

The aim is to make learned words feel less like isolated study material and more like familiar parts of the things, actions, ideas, and interests they describe. As your vocabulary appears in ordinary reading, your interaction with the target language becomes more leisurely, playful and personally relevant. Slowly the foreign language itself becomes more present, relevant and gradually less foreign.

When target-language words appear throughout the reading and routines you already enjoy, the language becomes a more regular part of everyday life—not something you encounter only during lessons or review sessions. That recurring, casual contact can help keep the language present, relevant, and easier to stay engaged with over time.

As your word bank grows, more of your familiar browsing becomes bilingual. That makes progress visible: you can experience the growing reach of the vocabulary you have learned in the reading you already do every day.

## Clear context, interesting reading

Learning words is easier when their context is understandable and fun. But at the beginning of language learning, there is often a frustrating choice: use simplified material that may not interest you, or turn the reading you genuinely enjoy into slow, effortful study.

LingoBlend takes a different route. It keeps the surrounding page in your native language, so the topic and meaning remain clear while words from your target language appear inside it.

This gives recently learned and not-yet-automatic words repeated chances to be noticed, recognised, recalled, and connected to meaningful situations—without requiring you to decode a full target-language page first.

## How LingoBlend works

1. Import the vocabulary you are already learning.
2. Choose how much to blend into pages.
3. Browse normally. LingoBlend replaces selected matching words in readable webpage text with entries from your target-language word bank.
4. Hover over a blended word to see the original wording and translation.

## A bridge to immersion

LingoBlend sits between vocabulary study and comfortable target-language content.

Learn new words through flashcards, courses, teachers, apps, or any method that works for you. LingoBlend helps those words leave the flashcard and enter everyday life, preparing you to engage more comfortably with books, articles, videos, communities, and other content in the target language.

It complements rather than replaces genuine target-language input. Grammar, natural phrasing, collocations, listening, conversation, and fluent reading come from using the language itself. LingoBlend’s role is narrower: help learned vocabulary feel familiar and ever-present before full immersion feels approachable.

## Private by design

LingoBlend reads and blends webpage text locally in your browser. No AI is used to process your browsing. Your page text, browsing activity, local word banks, settings, and local analytics stay in your local extension profile.

You can use LingoBlend with a simple local list of target words and translations. You can also choose optional vocabulary enrichment to push what LingoBlend can do even further.

If you choose enrichment, you explicitly submit your vocabulary list for processing. Your webpage text, browsing activity, analytics, and complete local profile are never sent. Enrichment can clean entries, check translations against dictionary data, resolve some ambiguities, and generate additional grammatical forms for broader, more reliable matching.

Enrichment does not use generative AI either—just good old-fashioned NLP techniques: dictionary lookups, morphological analysis, grammatical-form generation, and hand-crafted language-specific rules.
